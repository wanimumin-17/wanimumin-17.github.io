Privacy Policy

Cyber Site app · Effective 23 October 2026

This policy explains what the Cyber Site app does with information. The app is used only by the staff of Cyber Site, Opp. JLNM Hospital, Rainawari, Srinagar, 190003, to run the shop: billing, stock, repairs, suppliers and records.

Information the app stores

  • Shop records: stock, bills, repairs, suppliers and an activity log of who did what.
  • Customer details written on bills: name, phone number and address.
  • For second-hand devices bought by the shop: the seller's name, phone number and photos of their identity proof, kept as required for the shop's records.

All of this is stored on the shop's own device. We have no server, and we do not collect information through this website.

Google Drive backup

If the shop connects its Google account, the app uses Google Drive only to save and restore backup copies of the shop's records, including the identity-proof photos above.

  • The app asks for one permission: drive.file. With it, the app can see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot see any other files in the Google Drive.
  • Backups go to the shop's own Google Drive. Nobody else, including the app's developer, receives them.
  • The app keeps recent backups and deletes older backups it created.

Cyber Site's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising. The app has no ads and no analytics. A bill is shared only when the shop sends it to that customer (for example, by WhatsApp).

Keeping and deleting information

  • Records are kept for as long as the shop needs them for its business and legal records.
  • To stop Google Drive backup, disconnect Google Drive in the app's Settings, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files can be deleted from Google Drive at any time.
  • To ask what information we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it, contact us below.

Security

The app is locked with a PIN. Staff accounts cannot delete records or see purchase prices. Google Drive backups are protected by the shop's Google account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
